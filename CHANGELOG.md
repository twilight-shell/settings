# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Until 1.0, a minor version
may change how things work.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

The first release.

- The settings window, with a page for every component that describes its settings in a schema: toggles, ranges, choices, texts and commands.
- Pages drawn by hand: the palette, with palettes of your own and a color picker, the wallpaper with its pictures and live stars, and the plugins with their switches and folders.
